The 76 character voice clips use synthetic speech. They are not recordings
of the character's real-life subject and do not clone that person's voice.

Interaction sound effects are original procedural Web Audio synthesis made
for this project. No downloaded sound-effect samples are used.

Cycling mini-game audio is loaded separately from town audio:
- audio/cycling-pickup-metal.mp3, audio/cycling-pickup-chime.mp3 and
  audio/cycling-hit.mp3 are original procedural synthesis generated for this
  project by tools/audio/generate-cycling.py. No sampled recordings are used.
- audio/cycling-voice-pickup.mp3 is synthetic Chinese speech generated with
  the same Xiaoyi TTS style as the character clips. It is not a recording or
  clone of the character's real-life subject.
All four clips are distributed as MP3 files; cycling has no runtime TTS dependency.
